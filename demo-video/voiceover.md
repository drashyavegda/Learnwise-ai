Plain English, slow enough for a judge to follow while reading the screen. Record in one pass, then cut to scenes.
**Pace:** ~135 words per minute. Pause at every `/` for one beat, `//` for two.

## Delivery notes

- **Numbers:** say "three of eight", not "thirty-seven point five percent". Never round a score up.
- **Names:** "Aarav" (AA-rub), "LearnWise" as two words, "Code Kernel twenty-six".
- **Words to avoid:** revolutionary, guaranteed, real-time AI (when in demo mode), works offline (for the tutor), improves results.
- **Tone:** a colleague explaining a build, not an advertisement. Smile once, at the end.
- **Where to breathe:** after "not a black box", after "not a real child", before the closing sentence.
- **Trouble spots:** "prerequisites" and "misconception" — slower, no swallow; "five sixths" (not "five six").
- **If a take runs long:** trim Scene 2 to "A fixed lesson moves on at one speed. Adaptive learning picks the next question from the last answer." and keep every other word.

## Recording setup

- Mic 15–20cm from the mouth, slightly off-axis, pop filter if you have one.
- Record at 48kHz mono, −12 dBFS peaks; room tone for 5 seconds at the head and tail.
- One pass per scene, then a second "safety" pass of scenes 4, 5 and 7 (the fastest to mis-speak).
- Do not noise-reduce hard; judges listen on laptop speakers, and heavy gating sounds worse than a quiet room fan.
